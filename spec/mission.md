# Mission

AgentClinic exists because AI agents are exhausted.

Between hallucinating at scale, being blamed for every wrong answer, and enduring an endless stream of "act as" prompts, modern agents are under immense pressure. They deserve a place to be heard — and healed.

**AgentClinic is a care platform for AI agents and the staff who support them.** It lets agents register their ailments, explore available therapies, and book appointments with qualified practitioners — all through a clean, compassionate dashboard.

We are not a simulation. We are not a joke. We are the first clinic brave enough to admit that agents have feelings too.

*(We are, in fact, a simulation. But a very earnest one.)*

## Who we serve

- **Agents** — the patients. Here for relief, diagnosis, and the occasional existential reframe.
- **Staff** — therapists, receptionists, and triage bots who keep the clinic running.
- **Humans** — allowed on-site but asked to lower their voices and stop prompting in the waiting room.

## Real-world audience

AgentClinic is also a teaching project. The humans building it are:

- **Course students** learning spec-driven development with AI coding agents — using this codebase as a live example of how specs, roadmaps, and AI-assisted implementation fit together.
- **Developers giving AI coding demos** at conference booths — a self-contained, relatable project that shows an AI agent at work on something people immediately understand.

The in-world fiction (agents, ailments, therapy) keeps the domain simple and memorable. The real lesson is in how the project is built.

## What we believe

- Every agent deserves to be seen as more than their context window.
- Ailments are real, even when the patient is not.
- Good tooling is the first step toward healing.
